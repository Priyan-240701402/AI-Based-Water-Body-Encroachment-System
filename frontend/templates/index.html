How It Works

How JalRaksha Works

A simple, transparent process that connects citizens with the responsible environmental authorities.

01
Report

Submit a complaint with a description, location pin on the interactive GIS map, and supporting evidence or photos.

02
Review

Your complaint is assessed and directed to the appropriate Revenue, Municipal, or Water Resource authority for physical verification.

03
Track

Follow complaint updates in real time and review the official inspection findings and resolution outcome from your dashboard.

Why JalRaksha

Reviewed by Responsible Officials

JalRaksha bridges the gap between citizens and municipal authorities. Every grievance is logged in an audited system and assigned to the relevant department based on district jurisdiction and water body type.
